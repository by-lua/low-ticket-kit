# Molde de prompt — estático orgânico (ugly iPhone, digital + impresso)

Montar em inglês, numa linha só, sem aspas duplas. Exemplos reais: `OFERTAS/luciola-atlas-movimiento/criativos/CRIATIVO-*/intermediarios/prompt-v3.txt`.

1. **STYLE:** Create ONE single standalone photograph, vertical 4:5 portrait format, full-bleed, not a grid, not a collage. Ugly amateur photo shot on an iPhone by a normal person: slightly tilted crooked framing, imperfect natural window or room light, light grain and soft phone camera blur, no retouching, no studio light, no perfect composition, no AI render look, it looks like a spontaneous Instagram story post. Real ordinary objects, natural imperfections.
2. **SCENE:** lugar real da persona + quem segura o quê + onde ficam as folhas.
3. **DIGITAL:** `The smartphone: shows the attached page <TÍTULO> (<descrição curta>).` — 1 ou 2 aparelhos. Depois: Every device is face UP with the screen ON and clearly visible, the back of a device is never shown, hands never cover a screen.
4. **PRINTED:** exactly <3-4> loose printed sheets, each one a different attached page: sheet 1 is the page <TÍTULO>...
5. **PAGES:** Every screen and every printed sheet shows a DIFFERENT real attached page, copied exactly, never redrawn, never invented, never repeated: what is on a screen never appears on another screen or on a printed sheet. Keep the real page colors: <tokens do DESIGN-SYSTEM>. Printed sheets are loose paper spread around, overlapping a little, slightly curled, natural shadows, as if the person printed them and is using them.
6. **TEXT:** `Instagram Stories text sticker, as if typed by the person in the app, in <idioma/variante>.` Nada além disso — **não descreva cor, formato, fonte ou posição da caixa**: o modelo já sabe como é esse elemento nativo, e detalhar demais (cor branca, caixa arredondada, sem rabicho etc.) confunde mais do que ajuda (regra da dona, 24/10/2026). Box 1 exact text: ... Box 2 exact text: ...
7. **NEG:** Spell every word exactly as written with all accents. No other text, no buttons, no Instagram interface, no icons, no logos, no watermark, no emojis, no PDF icon, no mockup, no book.
8. **FORMAT REFERENCE:** the attached REF-FORMATO photos are reference ONLY for the table composition (phone/tablet + printed pages spread around a surface), never for the text style — say so explicitly if a reference photo shows unrelated text/stickers. (Bug found 24/10/2026, Atlas AR: over-describing the text box — and pointing at a REF-FORMATO that didn't even show that style — made the model drop the text entirely or draw a comic speech-bubble tail. Fix: name the element simply as "Instagram Stories text sticker" + language, nothing more, and let the model render it its own way.)

Expert/personagem: nacionalidade + ascendência + idade + roupa + ambiente de autoridade; rosto natural (visible pores,
no beauty retouching, mouth closed). Da 2ª peça em diante: anexar a 1ª imagem aprovada e pedir "the SAME man as in
the attached photo, same face…".
